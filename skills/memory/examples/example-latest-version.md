EXAMPLE LESSON. This shows the shape of a lesson. Delete it once there are 5 real lessons.

Find the newest version of a document before working on it. An attachment in an old email or chat is a
snapshot, not the document.

**Why:** on 2026-03-02, the pitch deck was edited from a copy attached to a January email. Two weeks of the
user's changes were lost, and the numbers on slide 6 were out of date.
**How to apply:** ask where the current version lives, or compare dates across the copies before you
start. Note the location in `ai-notes/facts/`.
